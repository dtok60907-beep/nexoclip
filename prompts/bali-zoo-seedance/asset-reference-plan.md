## Best Assets for Each Proposed Video

### Video 1 — Breakfast with Orangutan
Use directly:
- `BaliZooBabyOrangutan` for orangutan detail / baby orangutan visual reference.
- `BaliZooFamilyWalkway` for family styling, tropical path mood, and natural family performance.
- `BaliZooAreaMap` only for program/location planning.
- `BaliZooLogoStone` or `BaliZooColorfulMural` only for manual end-card/brand reference.

Need to generate or source separately:
- `BaliZooBreakfastLocation`: outdoor breakfast table near orangutan playground.
- `BaliZooBreakfastTable`: refined table props, plates, fruit, orange juice, linen napkins.
- `AdultOrangutanReference`: adult orangutan in habitat if the campaign needs an adult orangutan rather than baby only.

Recommended support image prompts:
1. Breakfast location plate: photorealistic outdoor breakfast table in tropical Bali zoo setting near orangutan habitat, warm morning sunlight, natural bamboo/wood seating, no people, no text.
2. Breakfast props plate: close-up of refined breakfast setting with tropical fruit, white ceramic plates, orange juice, linen napkin, warm morning light, no text.
3. Adult orangutan portrait: realistic calm orangutan in lush tropical habitat, warm morning light, natural anatomy, no human-like gesture.

### Video 2 — Elephant Mud Fun
Use directly:
- `BaliZooAreaMap` for confirming Elephant Mud Fun area exists and approximate program placement.
- `BaliZooFamilyWalkway` for guest/family tropical path mood.
- `BaliZooLogoStone` / `BaliZooColorfulMural` only for branding/end-card reference.

Need to generate or source separately:
- `BaliZooElephantMudFunLocation`: natural shallow mud pool in tropical Bali Zoo environment.
- `ElephantMother`: calm adult Asian elephant.
- `ElephantCalf`: baby elephant near adult.
- Optional: approved owner/guest reference near elephants, if available.

Recommended support image prompts:
1. Elephant mud pool location plate: photorealistic shallow natural elephant bathing pool in tropical Bali zoo, earth path, lush foliage, warm late-morning light, no people, no text.
2. Elephant mother/calf plate: calm adult Asian elephant and baby elephant standing together near shallow water, realistic anatomy, tropical greenery, respectful distance, no riders, no chains.
3. Guest observer plate: elegant guest standing safely on earth path observing elephants from a respectful distance, safari-neutral wardrobe, warm tropical light.
